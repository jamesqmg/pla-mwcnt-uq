Method-selection guide

Start here → Does the dataset span multiple processing routes?
If yes — use Hierarchical Bayesian (any n)
A flat model returns one pooled slope that misrepresents at least one subgroup. Partial pooling recovers each route's response (here a 2.2× FDM vs non-FDM difference) and stabilises small groups bootstrap cannot fit.
If no (single-route) — choose by risk posture
Bootstrap
Cost-sensitive · sharper intervals
  • Tightest widths (25.9 MPa) and ~350× cheaper per fit
  • Equivalent score to Bayesian above n ≈ 20
  • Overconfident: under-covers (PICP 0.846) on hard held-out points
Bayesian NUTS
Safety-critical · coverage guarantee
  • Prior-stabilised coverage holds at the smallest n
  • Conservative intervals (PICP 1.000) for qualification margins
  • Wider (28.9 MPa) and ~350× the compute
Default: above n ≈ 20 the flat methods converge in coverage and width — prefer bootstrap on cost unless a conservative coverage guarantee is required. The choice is a risk-posture decision, not a performance ranking.
